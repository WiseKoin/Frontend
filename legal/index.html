WiseKoin brings the moving parts of personal money management together without turning your life into a spreadsheet. Record what happened, understand the pattern, and decide what comes next.

01

Know where you stand

Your money, without the noise.

Keep internal wallet balances, income, expenses, and transfers organized. The overview turns your latest records into an at-a-glance cash-flow story.

  • Organize records by wallet and category
  • Review recent activity from one dashboard
  • Keep transfers separate from income and spending
02

Plan something worth saving for

Turn a big goal into visible progress.

Give each savings goal a name, target, and deadline. Fund it over time and see how today’s choices move you closer to what matters.

  • Track multiple goals side by side
  • See funded amounts and progress clearly
  • Record debts and repayments alongside your plan
03

A home for everyday records

Simple enough to keep using.

Sign in to an account-scoped workspace, add wallets, and build a financial record that reflects your routine. Recurring expenses can create scheduled ledger entries automatically.

  • Account-based access with security verification
  • Flexible wallet and category organization
  • Recurring internal expense entries
