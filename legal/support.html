Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Useful context for the features people most often need to understand.

Does WiseKoin connect to my bank account?+

No. WiseKoin is a personal recordkeeping tool. It does not collect online-banking credentials, hold funds, or execute payments.

Are receipt, voice, or notification suggestions saved automatically?+

No. These features produce suggestions for you to review. A suggestion becomes a WiseKoin transaction only after you confirm it.

What should I do if an AI suggestion is wrong?+

Check every field and correct the suggestion before saving it. If the behavior is confusing or repeats, send a bug report through the in-app Help Center without attaching sensitive financial information.

Is finance-app notification access required?+

No. Notification-based suggestions are optional. You can disable tracking in WiseKoin or revoke notification access through Android settings.

How do I delete my account?+

Use the in-app account-deletion flow and complete its password and email-verification steps. Logging out by itself does not delete your account or records.

Why can’t I delete a financial record?+

Some records cannot be removed while another record depends on them. Remove or update the dependent record first, then try again.
